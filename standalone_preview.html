REGULATORY DIFF

What Changed: Old Rules vs New Policy

1. Central Subsidy Slabs (PM-Surya Ghar)
Previous Policy (V1) 40% up to 3kW (~₹43,582). Complex benchmark costing with state caps.
New Policy (V2.0 - Active) Standard ₹30,000 for 1kW, ₹60,000 for 2kW, and ₹78,000 for 3kW+.
2. Net-Metering & Inspection SLA
Previous Policy (V1) No penalty on DISCOM delays; inspections often exceeded 60 days.
New Policy (V2.0 - Active) Strict 15-day maximum SLA for DISCOM inspection + deemed approval clause.
3. Disbursal Mechanism
Previous Policy (V1) Channeled via DISCOM accounts with 90–120 days turnaround.
New Policy (V2.0 - Active) Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into beneficiary's NPCI Aadhaar bank account.
